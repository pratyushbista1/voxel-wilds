01

Crafting

Put a log in the 2x2 inventory grid to make four planks. Four planks in a square make a crafting table. Place it, then right click to open its 3x3 grid. A pickaxe needs three materials across the top and two sticks down the middle.

02

Inventory & Survival

Right click to split a stack or place one item. Shift-click transfers stacks; dragging distributes them. Tools wear out and break. Hold right click with food to eat. Health regenerates when your food bar is nearly full. Falling, drowning and enemies cause damage.

03

Smelting & Combat

Make a furnace with eight cobblestone around an empty center. Put raw iron above coal to smelt ingots. Craft armor and a sword at a table. Equip a shield and hold right click to block attacks. On death, items drop where you fell and remain for five minutes.

04

Beds & Sleeping

Three wool above three planks make a bed. Place it on two clear blocks and right-click to set your respawn point. Sleep at night when no monsters are nearby to reach dawn. Death respawns you automatically after three seconds, at your bed or the world spawn.

05

The Nether

Make a 4-wide, 5-high obsidian frame, then ignite its inside edge with flint and steel. Stand inside to travel. Find treasure and mobs in fortresses and bastions. Wear gold to avoid provoking ordinary piglins. Beds explode in the Nether.

Creative provides unlimited blocks, flight and no damage. In Survival, inventory screens do not pause the world. Press Esc to pause safely.
